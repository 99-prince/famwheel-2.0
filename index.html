🔄 Workflow

How FAM WHEEL Works

A simple, transparent process from farm to market

01

Register & Verify

Create your profile as a farmer, buyer, or transport provider. Complete verification for full access.

02

List Crops

Farmers post their available produce with photos, quality details, pricing, and location.

03

Discover & Offer

Buyers search, compare, and send purchase offers or negotiate prices directly with farmers.

04

Confirm Order

Both parties agree on terms, quantity, and price. Order is locked and confirmed securely.

05

Arrange Transport

Request transport through the platform. Providers bid, get assigned, and pick up the crop.

06

Delivered!

Crop is delivered. Payment is processed. Both parties rate the experience. Done!
